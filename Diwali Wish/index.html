✨ Sacred Traditions of Diwali ✨

The timeless spiritual heritage celebrating the victory of light over darkness

🪔

Deep Daan

Lighting earthen lamps symbolizes awakening the inner soul, banishing ignorance, and welcoming spiritual illumination.

🌺

Rangoli & Toran

Vibrant geometric patterns and marigold garlands grace doorsteps to welcome Goddess Lakshmi and positive cosmic energies.

🪙

Dhanteras & Lakshmi Puja

Invoking the blessings of Lord Dhanvantari, Lord Kubera, and Goddess Lakshmi for everlasting well-being and prosperity.

🍬

Mithai & Harmony

Sharing delectable sweets and festive dry fruits among neighbours and family, strengthening the sacred bonds of love.

ॐ सर्वे भवन्तु सुखिनः सर्वे सन्तु निरामयाः । सर्वे भद्राणि पश्यन्तु मा कश्चिद्दुःखभाग्भवेत् ॥

May all beings everywhere be happy, free from illness, witness auspiciousness, and be liberated from sorrow.
